Teaching a Machine to Read Hand Gestures

For Project 3 I trained an image classifier with Google's Teachable Machine, using photos of our own hands, and tested where it works and where it fails. Try it below.

What I found

The teachable model I created is able to identify nothing, thumbs_up, and peace sign very well. However, it did struggle in two specific situations. The first is when I made a fist that wasn't turned sideways, and instead was straight up and down. Often, the model mistook this for a thumbs_up sign. Second, when I held my thumb up far away form the camera, the probability for peace sometimes rose, there were even instances where it became the most probable for the model. I believe that both of these errors come from the training data, as most of my thumbs_up photos were close to the camera, which affected how the model learned. However, I am not sure about the other issue, confusing fist and thumbs_up, as I uploaded many photos of a fist straight up and down into the model. The training graphs that I viewed on the teachable model page looked very strong, and didn't show these failures, as it was being measured on images similar to the ones that the model trained on.
